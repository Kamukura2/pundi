# CVFinance v7.7.3

## Annual Operating Performance

The right side of Prospect now shows a read-only annual operating snapshot:

- Annual Income = recurring client monthly income × 12.
- Annual Expense = monthly budgets × 12 + yearly budgets.
- Annual Net = Annual Income − Annual Expense.

Positive Net uses a green treatment and negative Net uses red. Events, Credit/PayLater, Stocks, and all History entries are excluded. The dashboard derives values for display only and cannot change Balance, Cash, Net Worth, Prospect, or persisted data.

No Supabase migration is required.

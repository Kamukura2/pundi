Pundi · Support

Support that stays simple.

Pundi is in controlled beta. Tell us what is unclear, what broke, or what would make your daily review easier.

Beta feedback

Signed-in users can use Send feedback inside the Pundi app to open this support pathway. Please do not attach screenshots, passwords, tokens, or finance records.

Open Pundi
For general support, email supportpundi@gmail.com. Signed-in users can also use the in-app beta feedback entry.

What to include

  • What you were trying to do
  • What you expected and what happened
  • Your browser/device category, without private session details

Safety

Never send passwords, recovery links, access tokens, API keys, or sensitive financial records through support. Pundi support will not ask you to disclose those secrets.
